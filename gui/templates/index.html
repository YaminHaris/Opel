Human-Readable Sensor Streams

What you are seeing: Raw sensor gibberish has been mathematically converted into real-world physics units.
• Angles: Real-time calculated head orientation (-180° to 180°).
• G-Force: 1.0 G is Earth's gravity. A crash or jump will spike > 2.0 G!
• Rotation Speed: How violently the head is spinning in Degrees per Second (°/s).
